## What this repository actually is

One learner's notes and hands-on labs, written while preparing for the beta exam. It is
a study aid, not a syllabus. Specifically:

- The lab structure **mirrors** the published section weights, but the labs themselves
  are my own construction and are not drawn from exam content.
- `PRACTICE_EXAM.md` contains questions **I wrote**. They are not real exam questions,
  not sampled from the exam, and make no claim about the live exam's wording, length,
  format or difficulty. A good score here does not guarantee a pass.
- Product names, API surfaces and model identifiers were verified at a point in time
  (recorded in [`docs/VERIFIED_FACTS.md`](docs/VERIFIED_FACTS.md)). They move.

## This is a beta exam

The certification is in **beta**, which means the product surface, the terminology and
the guide itself are all still changing. Some capabilities referenced by the exam guide

**Pre-GA / unverified**, along with the closest publicly documented equivalent.

**Re-read the official exam guide close to your exam date.** Content that was accurate
when written may not be accurate when you sit the exam.
